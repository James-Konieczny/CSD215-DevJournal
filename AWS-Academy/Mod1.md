## What Is Cloud Computing
- Cloud Computing is the on-demand delivery of compute power, database, storage, application, and other IT resources via the internet with pay-as-you-go pricing
- These resources run on server computers that are located in large data centers in different locations around the world
- When you use a cloud service provider (ie AWS), you are using the computers owned by that service provider

- You can now stop thinking of your infrastructure as hardware. Instead think of it as software.
- Infrastructure is thought of as Hardware. Hardware solutions are physical, which means they require space, people to maintain, physical security, planning, and a capital expense.
- Getting your own hardware has a signifivant upfront cost, you need to collect all the componenets and then maintain (fix what's broken) and provide resourses (electrical costs, aka power). Do you have enough storage to meet your needs?
- With their own hardware, a buisness must make sure there the resource capcity and sufficient storage to meet their needs. You need to buy the hardware to meet the needs when you are at your busiest, meaning when you aren't busy those things go to waste. You're guessing the maximum peak (the busiest time, highest traffic), so if you don't meet it, then you pay for stuff you never use. On the otherhand, you could under estimate and have too much traffic and things slow down and customers get mad.
- If your needs change, then you must buy new things and update things for a new solution

- With cloud computing, it's easier to expand and contract your deployment in order to meet your customer demand.
- With cloud computing, you can think of your infrastructure as software. Software solutions are flexible.
- You can select cloud services that best match your needs, provsion them, and terminate their use based on your demand.
- You pay for what you use, when you use it.
- New solutions quickly

- However, don't shut down having your own infrastructure right away just because cloud computing is popular. There have been cases where having your own infrastructure can save you money.

## Advantages Of Cloud Computing
1. Trade Capital expense for variable expense (Pay only the reasourser you need, save money on tech and mainting it)
2. benefit from massive economiews of scale (big company = more people put in money = you pay less)
3. stop guessing capacity (no guess on infascuture capacity)
4. increase speed and agility (only a click away, saves time and therefore money)
5. Focus elsewhere because no need to focus on cloud
6. go global in minutes, lower latency
