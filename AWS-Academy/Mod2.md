## Fundatmentals of Pricing
- 3 fundalmental drivers of costs with AWS: compute, storage, outbound data transfer
- most cases there is no charge for inbound data transfer (data you put in is free)
- data transfer between services in the same region is free

- You're charged on a monthly basis
- you pay for what you use
- no contracts, start and stop a service at any time
- no large up front expense
- no buying your own infascture

- Pay less by using more: get volume based discounts
- pricing is tiered, meaning you pay less per gigabyte the more you use

- As AWS grows, they focus on reducing costs by imporving operational efficiencies
- as of 2019, They've lowered pricing 75 times since 2006
- when a service is updated to something newer/better (higher-performing), there is no extra charge

- They even have custom pricing
- free tier for one year

## Total Cost Of Ownership
- Tradition Ifrastructure:
- On company's own computers
- Capital expense: facilities, hardware, licenses and maintence staff
- scaling up is expensive and time consuming, scaling down does not reduce fixed costs

- Costs are easier to esitatmate with the cloud
- To find out which is better option (cloud or personal infracsture), compare total cost of ownership

- Total Cost Of Ownership (TCO) is the financual estimiate to help identify dierect and inderect costs of a system
- There is server costs, storage costs, network costs, IT labor costs
- cost of hardware, software, facilities (space, power, cooling), upgrades, taxes

- Cloud companies make it pretty easy to estimate costs
- AWS Pricing Calculator: estimate monthly costs, places to reduces costs, price points
- They provide a 12 month, per month estimate
